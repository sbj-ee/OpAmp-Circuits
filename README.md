# Op-Amp Circuits

A collection of 103 operational-amplifier circuit schematics drawn with generic
op-amp, transistor and passive symbols: amplifiers, offset trims, oscillators,
references and current sources, instrumentation and bridge amplifiers, precision
rectifiers, sample-and-holds, filters, log/antilog circuits and more, plus a
Fundamentals section of basic building blocks. Each
circuit has a 300 DPI PNG and an SVG, plus short notes with its design
equations.

- Browse the index: [index.md](index.md)
- Gallery (GitHub Pages): https://sbj-ee.github.io/OpAmp-Circuits/
- Files: `svg/NN.svg`, `png/NN.png`, `index.csv` (number, title, section, paths)
- Sections: 01–25 Basic Circuits, 26–42 Signal Generation, 43–88 Signal Processing,
  89–103 Fundamentals (ideal model, follower, integrator and differentiator, first-order,
  MFB and state-variable filters, log/antilog, rectifier, comparator, Schmitt trigger,
  Wien-bridge and phase-shift oscillators)
- `gen/`: the Python generator that draws every schematic (see [gen/README.md](gen/README.md))

Circuit topologies follow National Semiconductor AN-31, 'Op Amp Circuit Collection'. The drawings, titles and notes are new work.
No endorsement is implied.
The Fundamentals section (89–103) shows standard textbook building blocks.
